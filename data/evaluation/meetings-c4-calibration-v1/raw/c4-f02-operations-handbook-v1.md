# Mirinae Community Tool Library: operating handbook v1

Synthetic, AI-authored fictional organization and people; not a real meeting record.

Issued 1 April 2026. This handbook belongs to the fictional Mirinae Community Tool Library (미리내 공유공구실). It describes routine responsibilities for a small volunteer service that lends hand tools and basic repair kits. It is an operating document, not a record of a meeting. Named staff and organizations in this source collection are invented.

## Access and reservations

Residents may request a listed kit online or by telephone. Both channels feed the same reservation register, and the recorded request order applies to both. A reservation becomes confirmed only when staff have checked the kit's availability and sent a confirmation. A submitted request is not a completed loan. Staff must not advertise a kit that is awaiting inspection or missing a required safety accessory.

The routine desk is open Tuesday and Thursday from 16:00 to 19:00. Saturday sessions are separate trials announced with their own hours and staffing. A trial schedule does not change the routine desk hours. Residents should contact the desk if they cannot attend their confirmed handover; volunteers record the change rather than marking the kit as collected.

## Handover and return

The standard loan period is seven calendar days from the recorded handover. Staff enter the due date in the confirmation and check the kit and accessory list with the borrower before changing its state to on loan. The borrower receives a pictorial instruction card. A first-time borrower also receives an in-person explanation and a check of the locking mechanism with a volunteer. A volunteer may pause the handover if a required safety item is unavailable.

Borrowers may return a kit at the desk or place it in the return box while the community centre is open on weekdays, 09:00–18:00. Receiving a return and approving it for another loan are different steps. The desk records the return time and transaction number; the maintenance lead checks the kit, accessories, and visible damage. Only the completed check permits the availability state to be restored. Items awaiting a check stay on the return shelf with a hold label.

If an accessory is missing, staff record what was received, keep the kit unavailable, and contact the borrower using the contact details already in the loan register. A volunteer should not invent a replacement charge or promise compensation. Any proposed financial rule requires a separately documented operating decision and notice to residents before use.

## People and records

Seoyun (서윤), the operations coordinator, owns the reservation and loan register. Dohyeon (도현), the maintenance lead, owns inspection records and release from hold. Nari (나리), the volunteer coordinator, prepares desk assignments and instruction materials. Minseo (민서), the records coordinator, keeps meeting notes, trial observations, and the action list. A task being assigned does not mean it has been completed; the owner records the result and date.

The register contains a contact route and transaction details needed to manage the current loan. Public trial summaries use transaction numbers and aggregate counts, not residents' names or telephone numbers. Working notes stay in the staff folder. Requests to correct a record go to the operations coordinator, who keeps a short change note rather than silently deleting the previous entry.

## Donations and review

Donation offers are entered in a separate register with the item description and listed accessories. Receiving a donated item does not make it lending stock. Donated tools stay apart from available kits until the maintenance lead completes an inspection and staff record an explicit stock decision. Volunteers may decline an unsuitable item without counting it toward service capacity.

Trial reviews consider handover queues, instruction time, return checks, missing accessories, and volunteer workload together. A change agreed for one trial date applies only to that date unless the decision explicitly extends it. The records coordinator keeps the earlier decision and links a later change to it. Permanent changes to routine hours or lending rules are issued as a new handbook version with an effective date; a simplified sign that repeats an existing procedure is an accompanying aid, not a new policy version.
