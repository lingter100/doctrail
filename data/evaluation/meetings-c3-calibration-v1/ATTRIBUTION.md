The public sources are documentation, not actual organizational meeting records.

The two files under `sources/synthetic/` are new AI-authored fictional Korean library records. They are labeled in each body; people, organization, dates and events are invented. `/root/c2_calibration_source` authored them for this dataset. They are not human-authored records, observed user behavior or actual library operating guidance. Human/user review count is 0 at source-author handoff.
